About Me

I am Ramon A. Merritt, a Computer Science student at Lamar University preparing to transition into a career in technology. My professional background is in refinery and marine terminal operations, where I have developed strong problem-solving, communication, and leadership skills. I earned an Associate of Science in Process Technology from SOWELA Technical Community College in December 2013. My experience at Valero Oil & Energy includes dock operations (2014–2019), dock equipment operations (2019–2022), Operations Supervisor responsibilities (since 2022), and training as a Dock Head Operator. In these roles, I have coordinated with coworkers and management, maintained operational records, supported scheduling, and helped teams complete complex work safely and efficiently.

My interest in computers has grown over many years, and I decided to pursue formal Computer Science education to build a stronger technical foundation. My primary programming language is Java, which I have used extensively in my Computer Science coursework. In my coursework, I have worked with Java, object-oriented programming, inheritance, interfaces, collections, sorting, file input and output, exception handling, and basic assembly language. These experiences have helped me connect analytical thinking with practical programming. I am especially interested in software development, IT, automation, and tools that help people solve real problems.

One of my goals is to combine my industry experience with new computing skills. I understand how important accurate information, clear procedures, and reliable systems are in an operational environment. I want to bring that perspective to technical teams while continuing to learn modern software engineering practices. This portfolio documents my education, experience, and hands-on work. The included study helper is a small interactive application built with AI-assisted development; it uses transparent, predefined responses rather than claiming to run a live language model. I plan to expand my project experience, strengthen my knowledge of databases and expand my familiarity with Google Cloud, and pursue opportunities in entry-level software development or IT. I expect to graduate from Lamar University in May 2029 with a degree in Computer Science.
